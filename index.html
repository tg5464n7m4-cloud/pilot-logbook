Pilot Logbook Support

Pilot Logbook is a flight logbook for iPhone and iPad. It works out night time, take-offs and landings, tracks recency, flight time limits and qualifications, and prints logbook pages in CASA, FAA and EASA layouts.

Subscription

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial, then the subscription renews monthly. You can cancel at any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. To restore a subscription on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the subscribe screen.

Your logbook

  • Importing. Settings › Import Logbook File accepts a logbook PDF printed by a logbook app, a CSV or tab-separated export, a spreadsheet, or a Pilot Logbook backup.
  • Syncing. Sign in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple Account; your logbook follows automatically.
  • Backups. Settings › iCloud & Backups keeps regular backups in iCloud Drive.
  • Exporting. Settings › Export Logbook (CSV), or print your logbook as a PDF.
